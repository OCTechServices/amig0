Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026 · amig0 / OCTech Services

amig0 ("we," "our," or "us") is operated by OCTech Services. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Account information — email address and password when you create an account via Firebase Authentication
  • Booking information — your name, phone number, event date, event type, headcount, and event location when you book a service
  • Payment information — processed by Stripe. We do not store card numbers. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data.
  • Deal redemptions — which affiliate deals you redeem and when, linked to your account

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm service bookings
  • To send booking confirmation notifications via WhatsApp
  • To connect you with the service provider you booked
  • To manage your deal redemptions and membership status
  • To communicate with you about your account or bookings

3. Information Sharing

We share your information only as necessary to deliver the service:

  • Service providers — your name and phone number are shared with the partner you book so they can contact you directly
  • Stripe — payment processing. See stripe.com/privacy
  • Firebase (Google) — data storage and authentication. See Firebase Privacy
  • Meta (WhatsApp) — booking confirmation messages sent via WhatsApp Cloud API

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers.

4. Data Retention

We retain booking records for up to 2 years for business records purposes. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@opcoretech.com.

6. Cookies

We use browser localStorage to persist your session and deal redemption state. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising cookies.

7. Children's Privacy

amig0 is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of amig0 after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at contact@opcoretech.com.
